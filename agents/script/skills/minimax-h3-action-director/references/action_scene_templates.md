# 8 Complete Action Scene Templates

Each template includes:
- **Narrative Arc:** Emotional and pacing progression
- **Standard Timeline:** 0.1s precision chart for ~10s execution
- **Shot Sequence:** Recommended camera angles and transitions
- **Director Variants:** How to adapt for 3 different director styles

---

## Template 1: One-on-One Duel (1v1 Focused Combat)

**Target Duration:** 8-10 seconds  
**Best For:** Two equal-skill fighters, martial arts showcase, high drama

### Narrative Arc
1. **Approach (0-1.5s):** Characters enter, position themselves, establish spatial distance
2. **Exchange 1 (1.5-3.5s):** First technique cycle—A attacks, B defends/counters, A defends
3. **Impact (3.5-4.0s):** Breakthrough moment, maximum force connection
4. **Reset (4.0-5.5s):** Recovery breathing, psychological shift
5. **Exchange 2 (5.5-7.5s):** Faster, more intense second cycle
6. **Climax (7.5-10.0s):** Final exchange leading to dominant position/stalemate

### Standard 10-Second Timeline
| Time | Camera | Fighter A | Fighter B | Audio |
|------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| 0.0-1.5s | Static wide, 12m | Ready stance, breathing | Mirror ready stance | Warehouse ambient, breathing sync |
| 1.5-2.0s | Slow push-in (1m) | Slight circle, testing | Mirrors movement | Footsteps, breathing quickens |
| 2.0-2.8s | Hold close A | 3-strike combo | Defensive blocks | Impact sounds (3x) |
| 2.8-3.5s | Slow arc tracking | Reset distance | Counter kick launch | Kick whistle, exhale |
| 3.5-3.8s | Impact hold static | Block, arm compression | Kick lands, force visible | Thud, pain grunt |
| 3.8-4.5s | Static on A's face | Deep breath recovery | Reset stance, circling | Labored breathing, ambient |
| 4.5-5.5s | Slow push-in (1.5m) | Aggressive approach | Defensive positioning | Footsteps intensify |
| 5.5-7.0s | Hold medium shot | Rapid 4-strike exchange | Defensive quick blocks | 4x rapid impacts, escalating |
| 7.0-7.5s | Impact hold | Power punch final | Block-counter attempt | Maximum impact thud |
| 7.5-10.0s | Slow arc pull-back | Stance reset, eye lock | Stance reset, defiant | Breathing sync, single cello note |

### Standard Shot Sequence
1. **EST_Wide** (1.5s): Establishing wide, both fighters centered
2. **ACT_Exchange** (0.8-1.0s): Fighter A's combo, close-up
3. **ACT_Counter** (0.7-0.9s): Fighter B's counter, arc tracking
4. **IMP_Impact** (0.3s): Force connection, static hold
5. **REC_Recovery** (0.7s): A's face showing effort, breathing
6. **ACT_Escalation** (1.5-2.0s): Faster exchange, multiple cuts
7. **IMP_Climax** (0.5s): Final strike, impact hold
8. **END_Reset** (2.5s): Both reset, psychological stalemate

### Director Variants

**Sofia Coppola Version:**
- Extend all holds by 50% (push 1.5s to 2.2s, impact 0.3s to 0.45s)
- Add shallow depth-of-field blur to background (faces stay sharp)
- Emphasize breathing and eye contact over speed
- Use pastel-tinted desaturation (warm amber tone)
- Remove rapid cuts; compress to 3-4 shots instead of 8
- Add slow-motion on impact (0.5x speed, 0.45s hold)

**Kathryn Bigelow Version:**
- Reduce all holds by 30% (push 1.5s to 1.0s, impact 0.3s to 0.15s)
- Handheld camera throughout with micro-wobble (2-4 Hz)
- Rapid cuts: 0.4-0.6s per shot instead of 0.8-1.0s
- Close-ups on eyes and faces dominate
- High-contrast desaturated environment (focus on facial expression)
- Every impact has distinct, layered sound

**Park Chan-wook Version:**
- Saturate colors dramatically (reds, golds, blues at 85%+ saturation)
- Extend holds for geometric composition (2-3s per composed shot)
- Slow lateral camera tracking (baroque motion)
- Red accent colors on impact/contact
- Asymmetric framing emphasizing thirds and diagonals
- Slow-motion arcs on rotational techniques (0.6x speed)

## Template 2: Multi-Opponent Combat (1vN)

**Target Duration:** 10-12 seconds  
**Best For:** Hero against group, action showcase, environmental use

### Narrative Arc
1. **Approach (0-1.0s):** Hero enters, sizes up group (3-5 opponents)
2. **First Exchange (1.0-2.5s):** Hero takes on first opponent(s)
3. **Crowd Engagement (2.5-5.0s):** Multiple opponents approach, rapid exchanges
4. **Peak Intensity (5.0-8.0s):** Multiple opponents simultaneously, environmental use
5. **Breakthrough (8.0-9.5s):** Hero gains momentum, opponents faltering
6. **Final Reset (9.5-12.0s):** Last opponent standing, dramatic standoff

### Standard 12-Second Timeline
| Time | Camera | Action | Opponents | Audio |
|------|--------|--------|-----------|-------|
| 0.0-1.0s | Static wide | Hero enters, ready stance | Group positioning (3-5) | Ambient, footsteps, breathing |
| 1.0-1.8s | Hold hero focus | Hero tests opponent #1 | Op1 approaches, testing | Footsteps sync |
| 1.8-2.5s | Close exchange | Hero 2-strike combo | Op1 defends, stumbles | 2x impacts, block sound |
| 2.5-3.2s | Quick cut, new angle | Op2 attacks | Hero side-steps, blocks | Attack whistle, impact |
| 3.2-4.0s | Arc tracking | Hero rotational counter | Op2 hit, stumbles | Impact thud, Op2 grunt |
| 4.0-4.8s | Rapid cut series | Hero vs Op3+Op4 | Multiple attacks incoming | Layered impact sounds |
| 4.8-6.5s | Dynamic handheld | Hero environmental strike | Op3 against structure/object | Object impact (louder than bodies) |
| 6.5-7.5s | Close exchange rapid | Hero rapid combo | Op4 defensive, retreating | 4-5 rapid impacts, escalating |
| 7.5-8.5s | Medium shot showing space | Hero breathing reset | Op5 (final) cautious approach | Breathing heavy, opponent advances |
| 8.5-9.5s | Close on faces | Final exchange setup | Op5 committed attack | Exhale, incoming strike whoosh |
| 9.5-12.0s | Slow pull-back | Hero victory position | Op5 down or retreating | Impact thud, breathing recovery, ambient |

### Standard Shot Sequence
1. **EST_Group** (1.0s): Establishing hero vs group
2. **ACT_Initial** (0.7s): First opponent exchange
3. **ACT_Crowd_Rapid** (0.4-0.6s cuts x3): Rapid opponent sequence
4. **ACT_Environmental** (1.5-2.0s): Hero uses structure/object
5. **ACT_Escalation** (1.5s): Multiple simultaneous opponents
6. **ACT_Final_Op** (1.5-2.0s): Last opponent focus
7. **IMP_Resolution** (1.0s): Final impact or victory position
8. **END_Breathing** (2.5-3.0s): Hero recovery, breathing

### Director Variants

**Kathryn Bigelow Version:**
- Handheld camera throughout, rapid reframing on each new opponent
- Cuts on every opponent entrance (0.4-0.6s per opponent)
- Close-ups on hero's face showing focus/determination
- Rapid camera repositioning following hero's movement
- Sound design: Each opponent impact has distinct frequency/texture
- Crowd reaction audio (ambient murmur, approaching footsteps)

**Chloé Zhao Version:**
- Natural handheld motion, organic camera following
- Minimal cutting; longer takes showing spatial relationships
- Close-ups on faces showing genuine exhaustion (not heroic grimace)
- Natural light only; overexposed backgrounds
- Breathing heavy and audible; genuine effort visible
- Documentary aesthetic applied to choreography
- Opponents shown as real humans, not targets

**Park Chan-wook Version:**
- Saturated colors, geometric framing of spatial relationships
- Slow lateral tracking showing opponent positioning
- Each opponent entrance framed as compositional moment (2+ second holds)
- Red accent colors on impact moments
- Slow-motion arcs on environmental strikes (0.6x speed)
- Geometric distance between hero and opponents emphasized
- Baroque camera motion; no handheld urgency

## Template 3: Pursuit/Chase Combat (Kinetic Movement)

**Target Duration:** 10-15 seconds  
**Best For:** Action across space, environmental navigation, momentum building

### Narrative Arc
1. **Separation (0-1.5s):** Pursuer and pursued move into space
2. **Chase Rhythm (1.5-5.0s):** Multiple environment transitions, increasing speed
3. **Catch Point (5.0-7.0s):** Pursuer gains distance/catches pursued
4. **Contact Exchange (7.0-10.0s):** High-speed fight during pursuit
5. **Resolution (10.0-15.0s):** Dominant position, exhaustion, standoff

### Standard 12-Second Timeline (Pursuit Version)
| Time | Camera | Pursuer | Pursued | Audio |
|------|--------|---------|---------|-------|
| 0.0-1.0s | Wide establish | Ready stance | Ready stance | Ambient, breathing sync |
| 1.0-1.5s | Slow push-in | Pursuer commits | Pursued evades | Footsteps, explosive exhale |
| 1.5-2.5s | Following tracking | Sprint pursuit | Sprint fleeing | Running footsteps, breathing |
| 2.5-3.5s | Dynamic handheld | Navigates furniture/objects | Vaults/dodges | Environmental impacts, footsteps |
| 3.5-4.5s | Arc tracking pursuit | Continues run | Jumps/changes level | Direction shifts, landing sound |
| 4.5-5.5s | Close on pursuer face | Pursuer gains distance | Turning to face pursuer | Breathing intensity, running |
| 5.5-6.5s | Impact transitional | Pursuer commits tackle/grab | Pursued caught/blocked | Impact collision, grunt |
| 6.5-8.5s | Dynamic exchange | Pursuer delivers strikes | Pursued defensive sprawl | Rapid impacts, struggling sounds |
| 8.5-10.0s | Medium shot | Pursuer dominates position | Pursued pinned/surrendering | Breathing heavy, ambient settle |
| 10.0-12.0s | Slow push-back | Pursuer reset standing | Pursued exhausted | Labored breathing, silence |

### Standard Shot Sequence
1. **EST_Setup** (1.0s): Both fighters ready, distance clear
2. **ACT_Chase** (2.0-3.0s): Running/pursuit, handheld following
3. **ACT_Environmental** (2.0s): Obstacle navigation, level changes
4. **ACT_Approach** (1.5s): Gap closing
5. **IMP_Catch** (0.5-1.0s): Impact of tackle/grab, body collision
6. **ACT_Ground** (1.5-2.0s): Exchange on ground or against structure
7. **IMP_Dominance** (1.0s): Final positioning, exhaustion moment
8. **END_Recovery** (1.5-2.0s): Both breathing heavy, standoff

### Director Variants

**Denis Villeneuve Version:**
- Very wide shots showing environment/landscape context
- Slow push-in during pursuer approach (from 20m to 10m over 3s)
- Pursuit shown through environmental navigation
- Patient pacing; tension through positioning not speed
- Deep depth-of-field showing spatial layers
- Sound design: Footsteps echo, reverb from environment
- Final exchange in wide shot with environment visible

**Kathryn Bigelow Version:**
- Handheld camera following at 60-80% of pursuit speed
- Rapid reframing every 0.5-1.0 second
- Close-ups on faces during pursuit (showing focus)
- Quick cuts during environmental transitions
- Handheld micro-wobble throughout (±2 pixels)
- Sound design: Heavy breathing, footsteps distinct, impacts clear
- Rapid cutting during final exchange (0.3-0.5s per cut)

**Greta Gerwig Version:**
- Choreographed pursuit with musical rhythm
- Color-saturated environments (vibrant backgrounds)
- Playful energy despite danger (not grimly serious)
- Slow-motion arcs on vaults/dynamic movements (0.6x speed)
- Musical rhythm integrated into pacing
- Composed shots (2-3s) showing movement grace
- Final exchange framed as dance-like despite combat

## Template 4: Defense & Counter Combat (Technical Focus)

**Target Duration:** 8-10 seconds  
**Best For:** Martial art demonstration, technical mastery, underdog story

### Narrative Arc
1. **Setup (0-1.0s):** Defender positioned, aggressor approaching
2. **Aggression (1.0-3.0s):** Aggressor launches attacks, defender only defends
3. **Passive Point (3.0-4.5s):** Defender takes heavy attack, appears losing
4. **Counter Setup (4.5-5.5s):** Defender finds opening, prepares response
5. **Counter Strike (5.5-7.0s):** Defense breaks into explosive counter
6. **Resolution (7.0-10.0s):** Aggressor down or reset, defender standing

### Standard 10-Second Timeline
| Time | Camera | Aggressor | Defender | Audio |
|------|--------|-----------|----------|-------|
| 0.0-1.0s | Static wide | Approaching | Ready stance, steady | Ambient, breathing sync |
| 1.0-1.8s | Hold on defender | Rapid punch combo (3x) | Defensive blocks | Block sounds (3x), impact clarity |
| 1.8-2.5s | Close exchange | Heavy kick incoming | Slip/parry response | Kick whistle, block contact |
| 2.5-3.5s | Aggressive handheld | Multiple attack sequence | Defensive movement only | Rapid attack impacts, defender silent |
| 3.5-4.2s | Static on defender face | Powerful punch attempt | Caught/absorbed on guard | Heavy impact thud, grunt absorbed |
| 4.2-4.8s | Medium shot both | Reset, breathing | Eyes sharp, reading | Heavy breathing, moment of silence |
| 4.8-5.5s | Slow arc around | Committed powerful attack | Footwork shift, timing | Setup for counter, wind-up anticipation |
| 5.5-6.5s | Impact hold | Attack extends, committed | Counter strike launches | Collision of counter vs attack, dual impact |
| 6.5-8.0s | Pull-back wide | Knocked back/stumbling | Standing dominant, reset | Aggressor impact sound, heavy breathing |
| 8.0-10.0s | Slow static close | Recovering stance, cautious | Defensive ready, breathing | Synchronized breathing, ambient tension |

### Standard Shot Sequence
1. **EST_Setup** (1.0s): Aggressor approaching, defender ready
2. **ACT_Attack_Sequence** (1.0-1.5s): Rapid attacks, defensive blocks clear
3. **ACT_Defense_Under_Pressure** (1.5-2.0s): Heavy attacks, defender holding ground
4. **IMP_Absorption** (0.7s): Maximum pressure moment, defender holding
5. **REC_Reset** (0.8s): Both reset, defender reading
6. **ACT_Counter_Setup** (0.7s): Aggressor's final commitment, defender's timing
7. **IMP_Counter_Strike** (1.0s): Dual-impact moment, counter connects
8. **END_Dominance** (2.0s): Defender standing, aggressor recovering

### Director Variants

**Sofia Coppola Version:**
- Extremely long holds during defense moments (2-3s)
- Shallow depth-of-field isolating defender's face
- Pastel desaturated colors with soft focus background
- Breathing audible, eye contact emphasized
- Impact moments held for 0.45-0.6s (contemplative)
- No rapid cutting; maximum 4-5 shots total
- Music: Minimal, only at counter-strike moment

**Park Chan-wook Version:**
- Saturated colors, geometric framing of defensive stance
- Slow lateral tracking around defender
- Composed shots (2-3s) on defensive positioning
- Aggressor's attacks framed as compositional elements
- Red accent colors on impact moments
- Slow-motion counter-strike (0.6x speed)
- Baroque camera motion emphasizing geometric relationships

**Chloé Zhao Version:**
- Handheld natural motion, organic camera following
- Long takes showing spatial relationships (5+ second holds)
- Natural light only; genuine sweat/strain visible
- Breathing heavily audible, exhaustion authentic
- Defender's eyes showing focus/reading (close-up)
- Minimal cutting; documentary aesthetic
- Counter-strike shown in context of space, not isolated

## Template 5: Staircase/Corridor Combat (Architectural Framing)

**Target Duration:** 10-12 seconds  
**Best For:** Confined spaces, vertical dynamics, tactical movement

### Narrative Arc
1. **Entrance (0-1.5s):** Fighter(s) enter architectural space, positioning
2. **Vertical Approach (1.5-3.5s):** Navigating stairs/corridor, tactical positioning
3. **Engagement (3.5-6.5s):** Combat in confined space, environmental use
4. **Climax (6.5-9.0s):** Maximum intensity in architectural constraints
5. **Resolution (9.0-12.0s):** Dominant position or escape through space

### Standard 12-Second Timeline
| Time | Camera | Fighter A | Fighter B | Audio |
|------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| 0.0-1.2s | Static wide staircase | Enters stairs mid-level | Positioned at top | Footsteps echo, breathing |
| 1.2-2.0s | Follow down stairs | Descends carefully | Watches approach | Footstep reverb, tension |
| 2.0-2.8s | Static corner framing | Positions at turn | Blocks further descent | Stance set, breathing |
| 2.8-4.0s | Tight angle stairs | A attacks up stairs | B blocks defensive | Impact on railing, echo |
| 4.0-5.0s | Low angle up stairs | A kicks, using height loss B | B counters rotation | Kick whoosh, counter impact |
| 5.0-6.0s | Arc tracking (limited) | A rapid combo against wall | B pinned briefly | Multiple impacts, wall contact |
| 6.0-7.0s | Push-in close A | A aggressive exchange | B defending, retreating | Rapid impacts (4x), escaping |
| 7.0-8.0s | Impact hold A's punch | A power strike connects | B block on railing | Maximum impact thud, strain |
| 8.0-9.5s | Follow action | A pursues B down stairs | B descends quickly | Footsteps rapid, gravity sound |
| 9.5-12.0s | Wide from bottom | B reaches bottom, turns | A descends final steps | Breathing sync, architectural echo |

### Standard Shot Sequence
1. **EST_Architectural** (1.5s): Establishing staircase/corridor, both positioned
2. **ACT_Approach** (1.5-2.0s): Vertical navigation, positioning
3. **ACT_Confined** (1.5-2.0s): First exchange in tight space
4. **ACT_Environmental_Use** (1.5-2.0s): Fighters using railings, walls
5. **ACT_Escalation** (1.5-2.0s): Rapid exchange using height/angles
6. **IMP_Impact** (1.0s): Maximum force moment, architectural contact
7. **ACT_Pursuit** (1.5-2.0s): Following through space
8. **END_Bottom** (2.0-2.5s): Resolution at space exit/bottom

### Director Variants

**Denis Villeneuve Version:**
- Wide shots showing full staircase architecture
- Geometric framing emphasizing vertical height
- Deep depth-of-field showing multiple stairs
- Slow movements through space (2+ seconds per stair section)
- Precision of footwork emphasized
- Lighting: Architectural shadows, dramatic lighting angles
- Sound: Footsteps echo clearly, reverb emphasizes space

**Kathryn Bigelow Version:**
- Handheld camera following up/down stairs
- Rapid cuts on each elevation change
- Close-ups on faces showing focus
- Handheld micro-wobble creating urgency
- Tight framing; staircase edges visible
- Sound design: Echoing impacts, footsteps rapid, breathing labored
- Rapid cutting during exchange (0.4-0.6s per shot)

**Lynne Ramsay Version:**
- Fractured editing between wide and extreme close-ups
- Close-ups on details: hands on railings, feet on stairs, impacts
- Rapid cuts creating sensory overload
- Handheld aggressive motion reflecting psychological pressure
- Textural sound design (metal railing, concrete, flesh impacts)
- Color desaturation increasing with intensity
- Subjective camera angles showing participant's POV

## Template 6: Environmental Use Combat (Prop/Setting Leverage)

**Target Duration:** 10-12 seconds  
**Best For:** Jackie Chan-style combat, object interaction, creativity

### Narrative Arc
1. **Setup (0-1.5s):** Environment established, objects present
2. **Object Acquisition (1.5-3.0s):** Fighter picks up/utilizes first object
3. **Object Exchange (3.0-6.0s):** Using object as weapon/shield against unarmed opponent
4. **Counter-Acquisition (6.0-7.5s):** Second fighter acquires object or new environmental element
5. **Dual-Object Combat (7.5-10.0s):** Both using environmental elements
6. **Climactic Impact (10.0-12.0s):** Objects collide or final environmental strike

### Standard 12-Second Timeline
| Time | Camera | Fighter A | Fighter B | Audio |
|------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| 0.0-1.2s | Static wide | Near shelf/table | Open stance | Ambient warehouse, breathing |
| 1.2-2.0s | Follow A | Picks up board/pipe | Approaches defensively | Object pickup sound (wood/metal) |
| 2.0-3.0s | Hold close A | Tests object (swing) | Flinches/backs away | Object swing whoosh, air impact |
| 3.0-4.0s | Exchange angle | A swings at B | B ducks/evades/blocks | Multiple whoosh sounds, near-miss |
| 4.0-5.0s | Arc tracking | A chases B with object | B retreating, blocks attempted | Chase through space, evasion |
| 5.0-6.0s | Static B moment | A strikes aggressively | B acquires defensive object | Strike impact (floor/wall), counter-grab |
| 6.0-7.0s | Exchange wide | B swings back at A | A evades, re-engages | Dual whoosh sounds, space control |
| 7.0-8.0s | Impact holding | Objects collide in clash | Both pulling/controlling | Object collision sound (high metallic) |
| 8.0-9.5s | Handheld chaotic | Rapid exchange both using objects | Back-and-forth strikes | Multiple impacts, object sounds |
| 9.5-12.0s | Pull-back wide | A final swing | B blocks/dodges/counter | Climactic impact, victory position |

### Standard Shot Sequence
1. **EST_Environment** (1.5s): Establishing space with objects visible
2. **ACT_Acquisition** (0.8s): First fighter grabs object
3. **ACT_Object_Use** (1.5-2.0s): Testing object, initial swings
4. **ACT_Chase** (1.5-2.0s): Pursuing opponent with object
5. **ACT_Counter_Acquisition** (1.0-1.5s): Second fighter grabs object
6. **ACT_Dual_Combat** (2.0-2.5s): Both with objects, exchanges
7. **IMP_Climax** (1.0-1.5s): Object collision or final strike
8. **END_Victory** (1.5-2.0s): Dominant position, exhaustion

### Director Variants

**Jackie Chan/Greta Gerwig Blend Version:**
- Choreography emphasized as playful combat dance
- Color-saturated environment (vivid background objects)
- Slow-motion arcs on object swings (0.6x speed)
- Composed shots (2-3s) showing object swinging grace
- Musical rhythm synchronized with impacts
- Whimsical tone despite danger
- Objects treated as dance partners not weapons
- All swings visible and clear (no blur, precision emphasis)

**Kathryn Bigelow Version:**
- Handheld rapid following
- Quick cuts on object acquisition (0.4-0.6s)
- Close-ups on object grips/hands
- Tension through handheld motion
- Each object impact has distinct sound
- Rapid reframing on every strike/evasion
- Documentary realism applied to prop use

**Park Chan-wook Version:**
- Saturated colors, objects become aesthetic elements
- Slow lateral tracking showing object positioning
- Composed holds (2-3s) on swinging positions
- Geometric framing of object arcs
- Red accent colors on impact moments
- Slow-motion object collisions (0.6x speed)
- Baroque camera motion emphasizing grace

## Template 7: Weapon Duel (Sword/Staff/Blades)

**Target Duration:** 12-15 seconds  
**Best For:** Martial art mastery showcase, geometric combat, line-based choreography

### Narrative Arc
1. **Facing (0-2.0s):** Both fighters positioned, weapons visible, distance clear
2. **Engagement (2.0-4.5s):** First weapon exchanges, testing distance
3. **Build (4.5-8.0s):** Increasing tempo, multiple engagements
4. **Intensity (8.0-11.0s):** Maximum speed, peak focus moment
5. **Breakthrough (11.0-13.0s):** One fighter commits completely
6. **Resolution (13.0-15.0s):** Impact moment, dominant stance, recovery

### Standard 15-Second Timeline
| Time | Camera | Fighter A | Fighter B | Audio |
|------|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| 0.0-2.0s | Static wide | Ready stance, sword raised | Mirror ready stance | Ambient, breathing sync, blade gleam |
| 2.0-2.8s | Slow push-in | Tests reach, blade movement | Mirrors blade position | Blade movement sound (air), testing |
| 2.8-3.8s | Static exchange | First blade contact | Blade blocks/deflects | Blade clash sound (clear ringing) |
| 3.8-5.0s | Slow arc tracking | Circling, blade line positioning | Mirrors arc movement | Light reflection sounds (whoosh), footsteps |
| 5.0-6.5s | Hold medium shot | Increased tempo strikes (3-4) | Defensive blocks/parries | Blade clashes (rapid, ringing) |
| 6.5-8.0s | Arc tracking rotation | Rotational attacks (spin moves) | Defensive positioning | Blade whistling, rotating body sounds |
| 8.0-9.5s | Impact hold frame | Power strike commitment | Defensive block holds | Maximum impact clash (resonant ring) |
| 9.5-11.0s | Rapid close exchange | Escalated 4-5 strike sequence | Defensive rapid parries | 4-5 rapid blade clashes, escalating tempo |
| 11.0-12.0s | Hold close on A | A commits breakthrough | B defending, strain visible | Heavy breathing, blade scrape (metal) |
| 12.0-13.5s | Wide pull-back arc | A final strike extension | B blocks/wounded/down | Final blade clash, impact/body reaction |
| 13.5-15.0s | Static victory stance | A reset, weapon control | B recovering/down/defiant | Breathing heavy, blade rest, ambient settle |

### Standard Shot Sequence
1. **EST_Duel** (2.0s): Wide establishing both fighters, weapons prominent
2. **ACT_Engagement** (1.5-2.0s): First blade contact exchanges
3. **ACT_Circling** (1.5-2.0s): Arc tracking around duel space
4. **ACT_Escalation** (2.5-3.0s): Increasing tempo blade exchanges
5. **IMP_Breakthrough** (1.5s): Power strike commitment moment
6. **ACT_Final_Exchange** (1.5-2.0s): Rapid final blade clashes
7. **IMP_Resolution** (1.0-1.5s): Final impact, dominant position
8. **END_Recovery** (1.5-2.0s): Victory stance, breathing

### Director Variants

**Park Chan-wook Version (Optimal Match):**
- Saturated colors with metallic silver/gold reflections
- Geometric line composition: blades create diagonals across frame
- Slow 1.5-2.5s engagements, emphasizing geometry
- Baroque asymmetric framing using thirds
- Red accent colors on blade/blood moments
- Slow lateral camera tracking showing blade angles
- Reflections dominating visual interest
- Slow-motion on rotational strikes (0.6x speed)

**Denis Villeneuve Version:**
- Very wide shots showing landscape context (cliffs, temples, desert)
- Deep depth-of-field showing multiple layers
- Slow push-in during engagement (from 20m to 10m over 3-4s)
- Precision and positioning emphasized over speed
- Geometric footwork spacing clear
- Patient pacing; long holds (3+ seconds) between engagements
- Sound: Blade ringing, wind, environmental reverb

**Sofia Coppola Version:**
- Pastel-tinted desaturation with soft focus background
- Long static holds (3-4s) at key compositional moments
- Shallow depth-of-field isolating blade contact
- Slow-motion on impacts (0.5x speed, 0.5-0.6s holds)
- Breathing and eye contact emphasized despite weapon focus
- Minimal cutting (3-4 shots maximum)
- Contemplative pacing; remove rapid exchanges

## Template 8: Desperation Final Strike (Exhaustion Showcase)

**Target Duration:** 8-10 seconds  
**Best For:** Climactic finale, underdog story, maximum drama

### Narrative Arc
1. **Setup (0-1.5s):** Both fighters exhausted, breathing heavily visible
2. **Psychological Moment (1.5-3.0s):** Eye contact, sizing up, final decision
3. **Commitment (3.0-5.0s):** Both commit to final exchange
4. **Collision (5.0-6.5s):** Techniques connect simultaneously
5. **Impact (6.5-7.5s):** Maximum force, winner emerges
6. **Aftermath (7.5-10.0s):** Victory position, exhaustion, defiance/defeat

### Standard 10-Second Timeline
| Time | Camera | Fighter A | Fighter B | Audio |

1. **EST_Exhaustion** (1.5s): Both fighters clearly exhausted

### Director Variants

**Sofia Coppola Version (Optimal Match):**
- Extreme close-ups on faces during psychological moment
- Shallow depth-of-field isolating fighter faces from background
- Pastel-tinted desaturation; warm amber tone
- Very long holds (2-3s) on exhaustion breathing
- Impact hold extended to 0.6-0.7s (contemplative)
- Slow-motion on final collision (0.5x speed)
- Minimal cutting (3-4 shots maximum)
- Breathing audible, emphasis on human connection

**Kathryn Bigelow Version:**
- Handheld camera with micro-wobble throughout
- Rapid close-ups on eyes during psychological moment
- Quick cuts on commitment (0.3-0.4s per shot)
- Handheld reframing on approach
- Impact hold shortened to 0.2-0.3s (quick)
- Rapid cutting on final exchange (0.3-0.4s per shot)
- Sound design: Heavy breathing, distinct impact, rapid audio cues
- Documentary realism applied to drama

**Greta Gerwig Version:**
- Color-saturated palette despite exhaustion theme
- Playful energy maintained (not grimly serious)
- Slow-motion on final commitment (0.6x speed)
- Composed shots (2-3s) on exhaustion/breathing
- Musical rhythm integrated (beats sync to breathing)
- Whimsical tone despite high stakes
- Characters shown with personality/humor despite fatigue
- Final collision shown with grace/dance-like quality

## General Tips for All Templates

1. **Timing Validation:** Sum all timeline blocks to confirm exact duration match
2. **Priority Placement:** Ensure climax beat is not in final 10% of target duration
3. **Director Consistency:** Apply chosen director's pacing throughout (don't mix timings)
4. **Martial Art Authenticity:** Ensure choreography matches actual technique (not just speed)
5. **Audio Layering:** Always include ambient + impacts + breathing + music (if applicable)
6. **Camera Strategy:** Match camera motion to action speed (40-60% relative motion)
7. **Observable Behavior:** Write all actions as what viewers see, not emotions
8. **High-Speed Compensation:** Apply one high-speed strategy per scene (push-in, holds, arcs, rapid cuts, or handheld tension)
